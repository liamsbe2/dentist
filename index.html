Signature Treatment

The art of
radiant white.

Our signature whitening protocol uses pharmaceutical-grade gel technology, achieving dramatically brighter results with zero sensitivity. A treatment unlike any other.

01

Shade Assessment — We digitally map your current shade and set your target with precision imaging.

02

Gel Application — Professional-grade whitening gel is carefully applied for maximum coverage and comfort.
